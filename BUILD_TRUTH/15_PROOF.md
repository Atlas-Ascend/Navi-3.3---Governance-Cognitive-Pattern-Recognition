# 15 — Proof

## Proof doctrine

NAVI produces analytical claims, not proof-class receipts. Proof remains owned by the estate's verification and evidence systems.

## Evidence chain

Every promoted PatternSignal must preserve:

- source observation IDs
- source-system identities
- relevant timestamps and analysis window
- detector ID/version
- configuration/version used
- evidence and counterevidence references
- confidence rationale
- executive disposition
- downstream work packet/build references, when created
- SECA/DevOS verification references
- ProofGrid receipt IDs, when issued

## Proof classes

### Analytical evidence
Generated by NAVI: observations, derived features, detector outputs, confidence, explanation.

### Executive evidence
Generated by JANUS/ODIN: disposition, routing decision, authority context.

### Execution evidence
Generated by Packet OS, Workforce Spine, MetaForge, or target runtime: commits, artifacts, logs, state changes.

### Verification evidence
Generated by SECA/DevOS: test results, audits, inspections, reproducibility results, claim checks.

### Proof receipt
Generated by ProofGrid: immutable/traceable statement connecting a claim to accepted verification artifacts.

## NAVI feedback

Proof outcomes are fed back to NAVI to calibrate detectors and track which signals resulted in verified outcomes.

## Anti-hallucination rule

NAVI must never manufacture a proof reference, claim a verification that did not occur, or upgrade a hypothesis because it sounds plausible. Unverified remains unverified.
